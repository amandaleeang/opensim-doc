# Tips & Tricks

A few things that make life in OpenSim easier, especially if you are coming from
Second Life or are brand new to virtual worlds.

## Moving around

- **Move forward/back/strafe:** `W` `A` `S` `D`
- **Fly:** `F` (toggle), or hold `Ctrl` while moving.
- **Jump:** `Ctrl` + `↑` (up arrow) or the spacebar while moving.
- **Turn / look:** click-drag with the left mouse button (look) or right mouse button
  (move/orbit). Mouse wheel zooms.
- **Run:** double-tap `W` or hold `Shift` while moving.

## Camera

- **Click-drag with both mouse buttons** (or `Ctrl` + drag) to orbit the camera around
  your avatar.
- **Scroll wheel** zooms in/out.
- Press `Esc` to reset the camera behind your avatar.

## Chat

- **Local chat:** just type and press Enter.
- **IM a specific person:** right-click their name in the People window and choose IM.
- **Nearby chat history:** `Ctrl` + `H` opens the chat history floater.

## Building & editing

- **Build / create object:** `Ctrl` + `3` opens the Build tools, or right-click the
  ground and choose *Build*.
- **Edit an object:** right-click it and choose *Edit* (`Ctrl` + `3`).
- **Move/rotate/scale handles:** use the colored arrows/handles in the edit window.
  Hold `Ctrl` to rotate, `Shift` to scale uniformly.
- **Take a copy / take:** right-click an object → *Take* puts it in your inventory.
  *Take Copy* leaves the original in place.
- **Permissions:** in the edit window, *Permissions* tab controls who can copy,
  modify, or transfer the object.

## Teleporting

- **Teleport to a person/place:** open the Map (`Ctrl` + `M`), search a region or
  avatar name, and click *Teleport*.
- **Teleport home:** type `home` in chat, or choose *Teleport Home* from the World menu.
- **Hypergrid travel:** if the grid allows it, you can teleport to another grid using a
  hypergrid address such as `hg.osgrid.org:80/Lbsa Plaza`.

## Performance

- If the world looks slow or stutters, lower the **Draw Distance** (Graphics → Draw
  distance, e.g. 128–256 m instead of 512 m) and reduce **Graphics** quality.
- Increase the viewer **cache** size in Preferences → Network & Cache if you visit the
  same regions often.

## Avatars & appearance

- **Change appearance:** right-click your avatar → *Appearance* (`Ctrl` + `P`).
- **Wear clothing/objects:** double-click items in your Inventory, or right-click →
  *Wear* / *Add*.
- Your avatar and inventory live on the **grid's servers**, so you keep them when you
  log in from any machine.

## Getting help

- Most grids have in-world help groups – search *Groups* for "help" or "newbie".
- The OpenSim community wiki: <http://opensimulator.org/wiki/>
- Ask the grid operator if you are unsure about permitted content or behaviour.
